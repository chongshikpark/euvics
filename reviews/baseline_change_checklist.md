# Shared-baseline change checklist

- [ ] Describe the proposed change and why it is required.
- [ ] Identify the requirement, design target, assumption, calculation, simulation, or measurement being changed.
- [ ] Link primary evidence or a versioned calculation artifact.
- [ ] List affected Proposal, CDR, configuration, table, figure, and generated-result files.
- [ ] Assess effects on interfaces, performance, verification, safety, cost, schedule, and risk.
- [ ] Record the disposition and owner in `decision_log.md`.
- [ ] Regenerate derived artifacts without hand editing.
- [ ] Build both documents and check citations, references, units, terminology, and numerical consistency.
- [ ] Obtain scientific, safety, cost, schedule, or project-owner approval as applicable.
