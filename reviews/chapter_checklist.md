# Chapter drafting and technical-review checklist

## Drafting

- [ ] State the section purpose and distinguish requirements, design targets, assumptions, calculations, simulations, and measurements.
- [ ] Define acronyms, symbols, units, energy conventions, angles, and RMS/FWHM or waist conventions on first use.
- [ ] State linear/exact-recoil, nonlinear, polarization, harmonic, aperture, and bandwidth assumptions with applicable results.
- [ ] Trace each material claim and numerical value to a primary/authoritative citation, approved table, or versioned calculation artifact.
- [ ] Mark missing information as `TBD` with an owner or a concrete question.
- [ ] Keep equations, tables, and figures near their interpretation.
- [ ] Avoid audience-inappropriate duplicated prose between the Proposal and CDR.

## Technical review

- [ ] A scientific reviewer has checked physics conclusions.
- [ ] A qualified safety reviewer has checked safety or shielding claims, if any.
- [ ] Responsible owners have approved cost and schedule content, if any.
- [ ] Terminology, symbols, units, significant figures, and cross-references are consistent.
- [ ] Citations resolve and directly support the associated claims.
- [ ] The document builds without new errors or unresolved references.
- [ ] Remaining `TBD`s and review boundaries are visible.
