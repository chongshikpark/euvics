\section{Superconducting RF Linac}

\subsection{Candidate Design Requirements}

The nominal roadmap input identifies a 3~MeV electron kinetic energy, but no
approved allocation between injector and linac exists. Energy tolerance, spread,
beam loading, repetition rate, radio-frequency stability, cryogenic load, and
availability remain TBD. A published low-energy continuous-wave SRF linac design
provides a methodology example for another application~\cite{CiovatiEtAl2018LowEnergySRFLinac};
its performance and cost are not transferred to EUVICS.
