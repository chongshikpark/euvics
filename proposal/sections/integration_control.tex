\section{System Integration and Control}

\subsection{Candidate Design Requirements}

Controls, synchronization, interlock, machine-protection, diagnostic, data,
facility, and safety requirements are candidates only. Their thresholds,
response times, operating states, interfaces, and verification methods remain TBD.

\subsection{Controls and Integration Evidence Required}

TBD (owner: project owner to assign controls lead): define the timing hierarchy,
control architecture, data and provenance model, alarm and machine-protection
functions, subsystem interfaces, operating modes, and integrated verification.
Laser-to-RF synchronization literature provides methods but not EUVICS-specific
tolerances~\cite{FilippettoEtAl2022UED}.

\subsection{Safety and R\&D Review Required}

Radiation protection, shielding, access control, and interlock claims require a
qualified safety review. IAEA SSG-8 provides relevant accelerator-safety guidance,
but applicability, local regulation, source terms, dose criteria, shielding, and
acceptance remain TBD~\cite{IAEA2010SSG8}. R\&D items require approved owners and
closure evidence.
