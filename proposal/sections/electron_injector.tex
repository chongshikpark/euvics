\section{Electron Injector - Superconducting RF Photoinjector}

\subsection{Candidate Design Requirements}

Candidate electron-beam requirements ELE-001 through ELE-007 cover kinetic
energy, bunch charge, normalized emittance, kinetic-energy spread, bunch duration,
arrival-time jitter, position, and pointing. Their numerical values and approval
status remain TBD. Published SRF photoinjector results document feasible operating
points for specific devices~\cite{ArnoldTeichert2011SRFPhotoinjectors,PetrushinaEtAl2020SRFGun};
they are not specifications for EUVICS.
