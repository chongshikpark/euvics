\section{Incident Laser System and Interaction Region}

\subsection{Candidate Design Requirements}

Candidate requirements LAS-001 through LAS-009 and INT-001 through INT-004
cover incident wavelength, pulse energy and duration, waist, normalized vector
potential, bandwidth, polarization, timing, stability, collision angle, observation
acceptance, and spatial and temporal overlap. Only the 800~nm nominal wavelength
is currently populated, as a roadmap-sourced assumed input with approval pending.
