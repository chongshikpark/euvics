# Superconducting RF photoinjectors and low-energy linacs

## Sources reviewed

- `ArnoldTeichert2011SRFPhotoinjectors` — review of SRF photoinjector concepts and implementations.
- `PetrushinaEtAl2020SRFGun` — primary experiment reporting a specific CW SRF photoemission gun.
- `CiovatiEtAl2018LowEnergySRFLinac` — peer-reviewed low-energy CW SRF linac design.

## Supported paraphrase

SRF photoinjectors and low-energy SRF linacs have been designed and operated in
multiple facility-specific configurations. Their performance depends on cavity,
photocathode, RF, cryogenic, beam-dynamics, and diagnostic conditions.

## EUVICS inference and limits

The sources support technology awareness, not selection. EUVICS energy, charge,
emittance, spread, duty factor, gradient, cryogenic load, reliability, and cost
remain TBD. Any transfer requires matched requirements and an engineering review.
