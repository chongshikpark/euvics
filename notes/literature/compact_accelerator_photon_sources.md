# Compact accelerator-based photon sources

## Sources reviewed

- `SunWu2011ComptonSource` — modeling and facility-benchmarked Compton-source study.
- `CiovatiEtAl2018LowEnergySRFLinac` — peer-reviewed design of a low-energy CW SRF linac for a different application.

## Supported paraphrase

A Compton photon source combines electron-beam, laser, interaction, and photon
acceptance subsystems. Published compact or low-energy accelerator designs show
possible architectures at their stated operating points.

## EUVICS inference and limits

“Compact” has no controlled EUVICS definition yet. Facility footprint, utilities,
shielding, cryogenics, beam dump, laser transport, and maintenance space must be
included before making a size comparison. No external source supports an EUVICS
footprint, performance, cost, or readiness claim.
