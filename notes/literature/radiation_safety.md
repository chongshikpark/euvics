# Radiation safety and shielding methodology

## Source reviewed

- `IAEA2010SSG8` — authoritative IAEA safety guidance for gamma, electron, and X-ray irradiation facilities.

## Supported paraphrase

Electron-accelerator radiation protection requires an approved operating envelope,
engineered controls, monitoring, access control, maintenance/testing, and shielding
analysis appropriate to the radiation source and facility.

## EUVICS inference and limits

The guide is methodological evidence, not an EUVICS safety approval. Applicable
Korean law, dose constraints, maximum credible beam loss, dark current, dump and
component source terms, occupancy, materials, penetrations, interlocks, monitors,
and verification tests require a qualified safety reviewer. No shielding thickness
or safety-performance claim is supported yet.
