# Interaction-region design and beam overlap

## Sources reviewed

- `SunWu2011ComptonSource` — parameter and collimation dependence.
- `KrafftEtAl2005NonlinearThomson` — general scattering geometries for pulsed lasers.
- `FilippettoEtAl2022UED` — timing and electron-beam diagnostic methods.

## Supported paraphrase

Scattering predictions require controlled collision geometry, spatial and temporal
overlap, observation definition, and photon acceptance. Diagnostics are required
to establish the relevant beam conditions.
